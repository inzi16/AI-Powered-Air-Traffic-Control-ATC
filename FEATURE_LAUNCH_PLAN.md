# Smart Air Traffic Control (ATC) 50-feature launch manifest

## Purpose and sequencing

This manifest records the **50-capability production roadmap** used to guide the revamp. Some foundations have since been implemented. [FEATURE_IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md](./FEATURE_IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md) is the source of truth for what is implemented locally, foundational, or still planned. The five tranches remain dependency-ordered.

SC-001 through SC-010 were the first priority because they establish the isolation, durability, command-safety, replay, contract, provenance, observability, test, and model-control foundations required by higher-level aviation features.

Priority meanings:

- **P0** — required for a trustworthy production launch or for safe development of dependent features.
- **P1** — primary product differentiation for the first complete training release.
- **P2** — expansion capability after the core product is reliable.

## Baseline when this manifest was authored - not counted among the 50

The following capabilities existed when this roadmap was authored and were therefore excluded as standalone launch-plan features. Consult the implementation-status document for the current repository state.

- A single in-memory `SimulationRuntime` with a fixed 5 Hz authoritative loop, REST snapshots, WebSocket streaming, session/sequence metadata, stale-data handling, reconnect/resync, and out-of-order rejection.
- A catalog/manual-coordinate airport-to-airport demo using a direct great-circle route, accelerated time, synchronized phase targets, landing, and completion.
- Basic MSFS/SimConnect ingestion when enabled.
- Synthetic nearby traffic and constant-velocity ownship-versus-traffic CPA alerts.
- Eight deterministic emergency categories, ordered checklist actions, emergency squawks, nearest-airport auto-diversion, and guarded resolution after required actions/landing.
- Regex/grammar-based parsing for basic altitude, heading, speed, frequency, squawk, direct-to, pushback, taxi, hold-short, takeoff, and landing clearances; issued clearances require a text readback before execution.
- Ollama-backed ATC chat with a deterministic fallback, plus browser PTT, STT, TTS, selectable audio devices/voices, synthesized alerts, and an optional cabin-style intro chime.
- A React cockpit with dark/light themes, responsive drawers, intro animation, flight data, event timeline, route and emergency modals, weather board, Leaflet map, and the selected fourth-concept radar style. The radar already has range rings, 10/25/50/100 NM scales, head-up/north-up modes, track selection, vectors, labels, CPA geometry, and conflict highlighting.
- Preset and text-selected emergency scenarios, session reset, Docker files, health/readiness endpoints, and the current backend unit/API test suite.

### Tranche 1 exit gate

Do not begin safety-sensitive procedure, trajectory, or emergency intelligence until two sessions are proven isolated, every mutation is revision-bound/idempotent, replay is deterministic, contracts are generated, provenance is enforced, traces are correlated, and SC-010 reports zero unauthorized AI mutations.

## Release-level measurement contract

Feature acceptance tests above are minimum gates, not marketing claims. Publish measured results per release for:

- Unauthorized AI/state mutations, schema-valid tool calls, readback false-accept rate, phraseology conformance, and retrieval citation support.
- Trajectory horizontal/vertical error, ETA error, conflict recall/false-alert rate/lead time, phase stability, and terrain/emergency safety invariants.
- Tick latency/jitter, WebSocket lag/reconnect convergence, rejected/duplicate commands, concurrent sessions, replay determinism, availability, RPO, and RTO.
- Accessibility violations, keyboard journey completion, mobile viewport coverage, and notification/caption parity.

Only convert targets into resume bullets after CI or a documented benchmark produces the result. A strong measured example would be: “Engineered a revision-bound, event-sourced 5 Hz flight-training runtime with zero divergent snapshots across _N_ million updates and zero unauthorized AI mutations across _N_ adversarial evaluations.”
